Text conversion

Newline Text to CSV Row

Turn one value per line into a CSV row. Commas and quotes are escaped automatically.

Paste your values to get started.Text stays in this browser.

How to use

  1. Paste one value per line.
  2. Choose whether to trim spaces and skip empty lines.
  3. Copy your result into your application.

Output format

Fields containing commas or double quotes are automatically wrapped in double quotes. Quotes inside a field are doubled. Each input line becomes one field, not a new CSV record.

apple
banana
cherry → apple,banana,cherry
